# لمحة AI (Lam7a AI) — logo concepts

Status: **awaiting the user's choice** (nothing approved).
Stitch project `15605789258921234837`, design system `assets/36af8002bd434fa8b291a1daf21f24ce` (not modified).

## How these were made
Stitch was tried twice with `generate_screen_from_text` (a 4-concept logo board). Both calls timed out and no new screen appeared in `list_screens` after several minutes, so **no Stitch output is used**. The final art is hand-drawn SVG (64x64 grid, vector paths). The wordmark is IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold converted to paths (shaped «لمحة» and Latin "AI"/"Lam7a"), so the SVGs need no font. Fonts for the board are in `_fonts/` (OFL); the generator scripts are in `_src/` (`node gen.mjs` then `board.mjs`, `shot.mjs`).

- Board: `logo-concepts.html` / `logo-concepts.png` (size ladder 16/32/64/180, reverse, mono, dark, lockups).

## Colours (from DESIGN.md)
Slate `#1E293B` (tile, wordmark), `#0F172A` (dark surface), white `#FFFFFF`; accent from the Fast mode: teal `#0F766E` on light, `#99F6E4` (fast border) on slate. Mono = `#000000`.
Variants: full colour = slate tile, white glyph, light-teal accent, slate wordmark, teal "AI". Reverse = no tile, on slate. Dark = white tile on `#0F172A`. Mono = single colour, no tile.

## Typography
«لمحة» is Plex Arabic Bold, no spacing/italic; reads right-to-left with the symbol first on the right, then «لمحة», then Latin "AI" (LTR) on the left. The English lockup is LTR: symbol, "Lam7a", "AI".

## Concepts
**A — Lam + pupil.** A heavy round-ended «ل» with a teal dot cradled in its bowl: the dot is the "glance" that lands on an idea.
16 px: reads as «ل» with a dot; the dot is about 3 px, still visible. Pros: closest to the current placeholder (smooth migration), simplest, strongest at small sizes, clean mono. Cons: the lam alone looks like a Latin "J" to non-Arabic readers; least distinctive.

**B — Lam eye.** The «ل» stem rises from an almond eye with a pupil: glance = look. 
16 px: stem plus an eye; readable, the pupil merges with the outline into a dark blob at 16 px. Pros: the most literal link to «لمحة» and the most memorable; reads as a lam too. Cons: busiest; an eye can feel like surveillance; eye marks are common in other brands.

**C — Bubble lam.** A chat bubble with its tail on the start (right) side and a «ل» knocked out of it.
16 px: the weakest: the lam counter closes up and it reads as a speech bubble only. Pros: says "chat" instantly, friendly. Cons: bubble-with-letter is very common (messaging apps), bubble tail in the accent colour is lost in mono, least special.

**D — Lam + glint.** A «ل» with a flat wide base and a slanted teal stroke above the stem, like a flash/hamza.
16 px: the glint becomes a 1-2 px sliver; the lam stays readable. Pros: spark-of-insight idea without a star. Cons: the glint is weak small and in mono; looks like an unfinished accent; can look like a "check/tick".

## Trademark and likeness
No sparkle star or known AI mark is used. Risks: B (eye) resembles generic eye/vision marks and some "view" icons, C resembles generic chat-bubble apps; both are common shapes, not distinctive. A and D are letterform-based and the lowest risk. None has been searched in a trademark database; do a formal check for the final choice, especially for lam7ai.com in the Arab-region classes 9/42.

## Recommendation
**Concept A**, with B as the alternate if a more expressive mark is wanted. A survives 16 px, is the clearest in single colour, keeps continuity with the current «ل» tile, and the pupil dot carries the "glance" meaning. If the user wants more personality, B's eye is the stronger story but needs the pupil/outline gap opened up for favicon sizes (a favicon-specific simplified version).

## Decision (user, 2026-11-12)

Concept **A** chosen. In the app: `BrandSymbol` (`src/components/BrandSymbol.tsx`) draws the glyph from `brand.mark` (path, stroke width, dot) on the brand tile, with the dot in `--color-brand-accent`; a brand without `mark` keeps its monogram. Icons and the share image are regenerated by `scripts/brand-images.mjs`, which now reads the symbol SVG from the running app. A formal trademark check is still to do.
